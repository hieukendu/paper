## Strongest Counter-Argument

The strongest challenge is that a label-wise ensemble selected over several development choices can make an aggregate gain appear broader than it is. The final result directly addresses part of that concern by separating the +1.5633 aggregate margin from the three per-label deficits, but it cannot establish that the same mixture will generalize beyond this fixed benchmark.
