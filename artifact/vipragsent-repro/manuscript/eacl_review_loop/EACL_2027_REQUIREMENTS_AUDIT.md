# EACL 2027 Requirements Audit

Target: **EACL 2027 Main Conference, Long Paper**, submitted through ARR.

The official [EACL 2027 call](https://2027.eacl.org/calls/papers/) records: ARR submission Aug 3 2026; all-author reviewer registration Aug 5; response Sept 14--19; discussion Sept 20--24; meta-review Oct 8; commitment Oct 11; decision Nov 12; camera-ready Nov 26; conference Mar 9--14 2027 (all deadlines AoE). It requires double-blind review, ARR submission, author reviewing, disclosure/ethics/limitations compliance, and the Responsible NLP checklist. The official [integrity policy](https://2027.eacl.org/calls/paper-integrity-policy/) warns that unverifiable references can lead to desk rejection.

Long papers are limited to 8 content pages at submission. **DEFERRED_TO_AUTHOR_AND_SUPERVISOR_PAGE_REDUCTION_PHASE**: no reduction is applied here. The final version may receive one extra content page.

Public artifacts must preserve review anonymity; direct author-identifying artifact links belong only in a camera-ready treatment. Authorship additions/deletions are prohibited during commitment (order changes are allowed).
