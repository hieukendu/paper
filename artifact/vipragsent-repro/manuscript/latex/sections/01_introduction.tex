\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Vietnamese pragmatic-sentiment evaluation must distinguish communicative phenomena from surface polarity.  ViPragSent provides a fixed, adjudicated setting with six pragmatic labels and a documented social-media source component, while retaining explicit data-governance boundaries.  The study compares recorded systems under a common evaluation rather than treating the corpus as a public, homogeneous release or the label set as a complete account of Vietnamese pragmatics.

The final research question is: \emph{On the fixed ViPragSent evaluation, how does a development-selected, label-wise ViPragSent ensemble compare with the evaluated best-tuned baseline systems, and where does its aggregate result differ from per-label leadership?}  The comparison is limited to the retained best-tuned PhoBERT, XLM-R-large, Sailor-7B, and Vistral-7B baselines and the promoted ViPragSent-Final system.  Historical ViPragSent variants and the repository's ablation and diagnostic artifacts are treated as versioned supporting evidence, not as interchangeable final-system results.

ViPragSent-Final is a frozen label-wise ensemble selected using five-fold development out-of-fold evidence.  It reaches 84.3883 macro pragmatic F1, exceeding Vistral-7B, the strongest complete baseline, by 1.5633 points.  This aggregate lead is deliberately bounded: the final system remains below the strongest baseline results for irony, idiom/figurative language, and code-switching.  The contribution is therefore an evidence-traceable aggregate-performance result, not a universal or strict per-label superiority claim.

The paper makes four contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item It documents a six-label Vietnamese pragmatic-sentiment evaluation with private-data, provenance, and access boundaries.
  \item It reports the frozen composition and development-only selection protocol of ViPragSent-Final, including its label-wise sources, probability blends, thresholds, and one-time canonical-test evaluation.
  \item It compares the final system with complete best-tuned baselines, reporting both its 84.3883 aggregate macro pragmatic F1 and all six transparent per-label gaps.
  \item It preserves a traceability boundary: stored predictions, configuration, and normalized artifact hashes are validated, but no independent training rerun is claimed.
\end{enumerate}
