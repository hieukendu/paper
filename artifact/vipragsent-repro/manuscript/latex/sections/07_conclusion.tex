\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

ViPragSent-Final reaches 84.3883 macro pragmatic F1 and exceeds Vistral-7B, the strongest complete evaluated baseline, by 1.5633 points.  This is the strongest overall aggregate performance among the evaluated systems.  It does not establish universal dominance: irony, idiom/figurative language, and code-switching remain narrowly below their respective best-tuned baseline leaders.  Future work should target these three labels, test the stability of the development-selected mixture, and address the remaining data-governance and access limitations.  The reported traceability supports inspection of the stored evidence, not an independent full training reproduction.
