\section{Systems and Evaluation}
\label{sec:systems-evaluation}

\subsection{Compared Systems and Final System}

The final comparison uses best-tuned PhoBERT, XLM-R-large, Sailor-7B, and Vistral-7B systems as complete baselines.  These are compared with \textbf{ViPragSent-Final}, the promoted continuation of the ViPragSent line.  Historical original, previous-optimized, and incumbent ViPragSent rows are retained only as development progression, not as independent external baselines.

ViPragSent-Final is not a single ordinary encoder.  It is a frozen label-wise prediction ensemble: three-seed ViSoBERT supplies implicit sentiment and sarcasm; a 0.6 ViSoBERT / 0.4 PhoBERT probability blend supplies irony and code-switching; uniform three-seed ViSoBERT supplies idiom/figurative language; and a targeted uniform three-seed component supplies mocking.  This composition is reported to make the final system auditable, not to attribute its performance to any individual component.

\subsection{Development Selection and Test Evaluation}

Source choices and thresholds were selected with deterministic five-fold multilabel out-of-fold development evaluation.  The stored target-label selection objective is mean target-label F1 minus $0.25$ times the standard deviation across folds.  After this development-only selection, thresholds were refit on the full development data (0.55, 0.87, 0.96, 0.99, 0.50, and 0.35 for implicit sentiment, sarcasm, irony, idiom/figurative language, code-switching, and mocking, respectively), and the configuration was frozen.

The frozen system was then evaluated once on the canonical 2,000-record test partition.  The final-selection artifacts explicitly record that test labels were not used for selection.  The test prediction IDs exactly match the canonical test IDs.  The stored promotion rule requires a strictly higher macro pragmatic F1 than the incumbent, an improved target, and no pragmatic-label drop greater than 0.10 points.

Macro pragmatic F1 is the equal-weight mean of six label-level binary macro-F1 values, where each label-level value averages its negative-class and positive-class F1.  Consequently, the aggregate score measures balance across the six specified pragmatic labels; it does not imply that the same system leads every label.

\subsection{Reproducibility and Scope}

The promoted bundle records configuration, source assignments, thresholds, prediction files, artifact hashes, and an archived incumbent.  Its 53 indexed files match after normalization of the local Windows CRLF transport conversion to the index's LF form.  The nine checkpoint paths and hashes are recorded, but the checkpoint files are not present locally.  The present study therefore provides artifact-level traceability and validation of stored predictions, rather than an independent full training reproduction.

Legacy ablation, source-stratified, low-resource, calibration, and external-diagnostic analyses are retained only with their original system-version scope.  They are not evidence about the final ensemble unless they are explicitly re-evaluated for that system.
