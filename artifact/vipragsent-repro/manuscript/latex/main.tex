\title{\headingstyle ViPragSent-Final: A Development-Selected Ensemble for Vietnamese Pragmatic Sentiment Evaluation}
\author{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Pragmatic interpretation in Vietnamese-language text can depend on signals beyond surface polarity. We evaluate six pragmatic phenomena in the fixed ViPragSent setting, which has a documented social-media source component and a private-data boundary. ViPragSent-Final is a frozen label-wise ensemble selected with five-fold development out-of-fold evidence: it combines three-seed ViSoBERT and PhoBERT sources, probability blends for irony and code-switching, and a targeted mocking component. On one post-freeze canonical-test evaluation, it reaches 84.39 macro pragmatic F1, 1.56 points above Vistral-7B, the strongest complete best-tuned baseline. The aggregate lead does not establish strict per-label dominance: the final system remains 0.40 points below the strongest irony result, 0.11 below the strongest idiom/figurative-language result, and 0.13 below the strongest code-switching result. Stored predictions, configuration, and artifact hashes are traceable, but this is not an independent training reproduction; raw source text remains private.
\end{abstract}
